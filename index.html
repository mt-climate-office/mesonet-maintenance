    Mesonet Station Maintenance

    Maintenance visits across the Montana Mesonet network

    What this shows

    Only HydroMet stations require a Maintenance visit at least once per calendar year (a Repair visit does not count): they show green once they have had one this year, and red if overdue. Stations installed this year are exempt (shown blue). AgriMet stations are visited as needed — neutral, never marked overdue. The effective date of a visit is its Visit Date.

    Color modes

    • Compliance — HydroMet visited-this-year vs. overdue; AgriMet as-needed.
    • Time since — how long since the last Maintenance visit (all networks).
    • Trip type — the type of the most recent completed visit.

    How to use it

    • Search — press / to jump there; ↑/↓ + Enter to pick.
    • Networks — chips isolate HydroMet / AgriMet.
    • Legend — click a row to hide a category; double-click (or Shift+Enter) to isolate.
    • Hover a dot for its status; click for the full visit history + dashboard link.
    • Numbered dots — multiple stations share that location. Hover or tap to fan them out; click one for its details.
    • Esc closes any popup or fan-out.
